State monitoring playbook

State Risk Overview: How to Analyse

Use State Risk Overview to identify quality issues affecting the greatest number of facilities, then narrow the result to the location, facility type, department and checkpoint that needs action. It is a prioritisation tool—not a replacement for facility verification or clinical review.

1
Start here: Open State Risk Overview, check Assessment Coverage, then click the highest Area of Concern to see its actual weak checkpoints.

What the page answers

  • Which quality concern is weak in the most facilities?
  • Which district, facility type and department should be reviewed first?
  • Which exact checklist checkpoints explain the concern?
  • Which facilities should receive a focused follow-up or support visit?

Before you start

  • Select the reporting scope using search and drill-down filters.
  • Confirm that the assessment data is current enough for the review meeting.
  • Check Assessment Coverage before comparing locations or facility types.
  • Use the minimum affected-facilities field to hide isolated issues when preparing a State-level review.

Recommended analysis workflow

Follow the coloured steps in order. Each click on the overview narrows the result; use “Show All…” to remove a filter.

1

Check coverage

Read Assessment Coverage. A concern seen in 20 of 20 assessed facilities is stronger evidence than one seen in 20 of 2,000 facilities.

2

Choose the priority Area of Concern

Start with the concern having the most affected facilities. Click it to filter Priority Risk Details to its real Health checkpoints.

3

Locate the risk

Click a district in Geographic Risk Breakdown. Then compare facility types to decide whether the issue is local, type-specific or widespread.

4

Identify the responsible service area

Click the relevant department. The Priority Risk Details table now lists the actual checkpoint text, Department/Package and Area of Concern.

5

Act and verify

Download the affected facilities for the checkpoint, assign an action owner and due date, then re-assess or verify evidence after corrective action.

How to interpret each measure

MeasureMeaningUse it for
Affected facilitiesFacilities with one or more responses in the lowest configured score band.Primary prioritisation measure for State and district action.
Low-score checkpointsDistinct checklist items contributing to the risk.Estimate the breadth of the quality problem.
Low-score responsesTotal recorded weak responses.Supporting evidence when two groups affect similar numbers of facilities.
Assessment coverageAssessed facilities divided by total facilities in the active scope.Judge confidence in the comparison and identify data-collection gaps.

Using the drill-downs

  • Area of Concern: filters Priority Risk Details to related checkpoints.
  • District: filters all comparisons to that district.
  • Facility Type: identifies whether risk is concentrated in PHC, CHC, DH, AAM-SC or another type.
  • Department: narrows the checkpoint list to the responsible service area.
  • Show All… buttons: remove only the selected drill-down filter.

Suggested review decisions

  • Widespread: many districts and facility types affected—plan a State programme action.
  • District-specific: one or few districts dominate—assign a district support visit.
  • Type-specific: one facility type dominates—review its standards, supplies, staffing and training.
  • Department-specific: focus action with the concerned department lead.

From analysis to action

For every selected priority, record: Area of Concern, checkpoint, affected facilities, responsible district/programme team, corrective action, due date, evidence required and verification date. Link the work to Gap Analysis and Action Plan so the next review can distinguish closed actions from repeated risk.

Good practice: compare the same scope and score band at each review. A drop in affected facilities is meaningful only when assessment coverage has stayed the same or improved.
Caution: Do not rank a district as low-performing only from raw response counts. Use affected facilities and coverage together, then verify results with the underlying facility list and evidence.
