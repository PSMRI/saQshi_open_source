SaQshi makes quality improvement visible and actionable.

SaQshi is an open-source digital platform for structured assessment, continuous quality improvement, monitoring and reporting.

Built for practical public-service quality work

SaQshi brings assessment, gap analysis, action planning, performance monitoring, certification tracking and reports into one workflow. Its configurable design supports programme-specific frameworks while keeping the focus on evidence, accountability and continuous improvement.

The platform is maintained by the Tech4Gov team at Piramal Swasthya and is available as an open-source project.
